| Microphone | Type | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

### Acoustic Treatment

> Room acoustics matter more than most gear upgrades. A $50 acoustic investment beats a $500 mic upgrade in a bad room.

**Panels & Products:**
- **Acoustic foam panels** — Affordable, reduces flutter echo (brands: Acoustimac, Foam Factory)
- **Bass traps** — Corner placement absorbs low-frequency room modes
- **Diffusion panels** — Scatter sound rather than absorbing; preserves natural liveliness
- **Reflection filter** — Placed behind the mic; Rode PSA1 + Reflexion Filter combo is popular
- **Portable vocal booths** — SE Electronics Reflexion Filter Pro, Kaotica Eyeball

**DIY Solutions (Zero Cost):**
- Record inside a walk-in closet surrounded by hanging clothes
- Hang heavy blankets or moving pads on walls
- Place a thick rug under your desk setup
- Turn off HVAC, fans, and refrigerators before recording
- Record at night when ambient noise is lowest

### Remote Recording

> The golden rule: **always record locally on both sides** and sync in post. Never rely solely on call quality.

| Tool | Audio Quality | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| **Riverside.fm** | Up to 48kHz WAV | Local recording + 4K video, separate tracks per guest |
| **Squadcast** | 48kHz WAV | SOC 2 compliant, progressive upload backup |
| **Zencastr** | 16-bit WAV | Browser-based, automatic post-production |
| **Cleanfeed** | Broadcast quality | Ultra-low latency, used by major radio networks |
| **StreamYard** | Good | Multi-platform live streaming + recording |
| **Zoom** (+ local record) | Compressed | Ubiquitous but use only as backup; record locally via Audacity simultaneously |

> 💡 **Pro workflow**: Use Riverside or Squadcast for the call. Have guests record locally with Audacity as a backup. Sync tracks in post using the clap method or auto-align in iZotope RX.

- Write **detailed show notes** for every episode (300–500 words minimum with timestamps)
- Include **target search keywords** in episode titles and descriptions — think "what would a new listener type to find this?"
- Transcribe every episode and **publish as a blog post** (doubles as SEO content)
- Submit to **Podchaser** and fully complete your profile (acts like an IMDb page for podcasts)

## 📝 Content Planning & Workflow

### Episode Planning Framework

```
📋 Pre-Production Checklist
├── 📌 Episode Strategy
│   ├── Target listener persona (who is this episode for?)
│   ├── Core takeaway (one sentence: what will they learn/feel?)
│   └── Call to action (what do you want them to do after?)
├── 🎬 Format Decision
│   ├── Solo / Co-host / Interview / Panel / Narrative
│   ├── Episode length (15–20 min tight / 45–60 min standard / 2hr+ long-form)
│   └── Publishing frequency (daily, weekly, bi-weekly)
├── 📅 Content Calendar
│   ├── Topic batching (record 4 episodes in one day)
│   ├── Guest pipeline (6–8 weeks out)
│   └── Seasonal/timely hooks
└── 🔄 Production SOP
    ├── Pre-call: send prep doc to guests
    ├── Recording: run levels check, record room tone
    └── Post: edit → master → write notes → schedule → promote
```

### Standard Episode Structure

```
[0:00]   Intro music + show opener (30 sec)
[0:30]   Hook — most compelling moment or question (30–60 sec)
[1:30]   Host intro + episode teaser
[3:00]   Guest intro / topic intro
[5:00]   Core content / interview
[XX:XX]  Mid-roll ad break (natural pause point)
[XX:XX]  Continued content
[End-3]  Key takeaways / summary
[End-1]  CTA: subscribe, leave a review, join community, share
[End]    Outro music (15–30 sec)
```

### Interview Techniques

- **FORD Framework** — Family, Occupation, Recreation, Dreams — universal rapport-builder
- **The Iceberg Question** — After any answer, ask "What's underneath that?" or "Why does that matter to you?"
- **Pre-interview brief** — Share 5–7 questions in advance; better answers, less dead air
- **The Pregnant Pause** — Stay silent after an answer; guests fill silence with their most honest thoughts
- **"Last thing" close** — End with: "Is there anything important we didn't cover?" — often produces gold

## 🧭 Launch Checklist

```
✅ Pre-Production (4–6 weeks before launch)
□ Define your show concept, niche, and target listener
□ Validate the idea (are there advertisers in this space? an active audience?)
□ Choose a memorable show name (check trademark, domain, social handles)
□ Design cover art (3000×3000px, JPEG, under 500KB)
□ Choose a hosting platform and create account
□ Record a trailer episode (60–90 seconds: hook + what/who/why/when)
□ Record 3–5 episodes before launch (avoid "podcasting off a cliff")
□ Write compelling show description with target keywords
□ Set up show website or landing page

✅ Technical Setup
□ Configure audio interface and test recording chain
□ Set loudness target: -16 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP true peak
□ Create consistent intro/outro template
□ Set up ID3 tags (title, author, description, cover art) on all files
□ Generate RSS feed via hosting platform

✅ Distribution (2 weeks before launch)
□ Submit to Apple Podcasts Connect (allow 2–5 business days for review)
□ Submit to Spotify for Podcasters (usually 24–48 hours)
□ Submit to Amazon Music / Audible Podcasts
□ Submit to iHeartRadio
□ Claim and complete your Podchaser profile
□ Register with Podtrac (for download measurement certification)

✅ Launch Week
□ Publish your trailer + 3 episodes simultaneously on Day 1
□ Email your existing network personally (not a mass blast)
□ Notify guests and provide shareable clip assets
□ Create Audiograms for each episode
□ Post across all social channels
□ Ask your 20 most supportive contacts for an Apple Podcasts review
□ Submit to "New & Noteworthy" via Podcasts Connect editorial form

✅ Ongoing Operations
□ Batch record (2–4 episodes per session)
□ Publish show notes within 24 hours of episode drop
□ Respond to every listener comment and DM in the first 6 months
□ Monthly: review analytics and identify best-performing content
□ Quarterly: update show description and artwork if needed
□ Pursue 1 new podcast as a guest per month
```
